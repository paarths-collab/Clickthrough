# Tasklet - product brief

Tasklet is a small task tracker for small teams. A person signs up, makes
projects, puts tasks in them and ticks them off.

## Who uses it
- **Owner** - creates projects and tasks, invites teammates.
- **New user** - has just signed up and has nothing yet.

## What a user can do
1. **Sign up** with name, email and password. After signing up you land on
   your dashboard, already logged in.
2. **Log in / log out.** If you forget your password you can reset it by
   email from the login page.
3. **Projects.** Create a project by giving it a name. If you leave the name
   empty you are told a name is required. You see a list of your projects.
4. **Tasks.** Add a task to a project from the project page, or from the
   quick-add box on the dashboard. Tick a task to mark it done; it stays done
   when you come back. Delete a task you no longer need.
5. **My Tasks.** One page listing every task across your projects. A new
   user with no tasks sees a friendly "no tasks yet" message.
6. **Team.** Invite teammates by email from the Team page in the sidebar.
7. **Settings.** Change your display name. The new name shows everywhere
   and is still there next time you log in.

## Privacy
Nobody else can see your projects or tasks.
